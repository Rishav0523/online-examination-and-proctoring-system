Before you begin

General examination instructions

These rules apply to every examination hosted on this portal. Individual examinations may include additional instructions that take precedence.

Read carefully before starting an examination

You will be asked to confirm that you have read and understood these instructions.

  1. 1Read all examination instructions carefully before starting.
  2. 2Ensure that you have a stable internet connection.
  3. 3Use a supported desktop browser for the best experience.
  4. 4Allow webcam access when it is requested by the examination.
  5. 5Keep your face visible during monitored examinations.
  6. 6Avoid switching browser tabs during the examination.
  7. 7Do not exit fullscreen mode unless you are permitted to do so.
  8. 8Read every question carefully before choosing an answer.
  9. 9Review your answers before final submission.
  10. 10Submit before the examination timer expires.
  11. 11The system may automatically submit an examination after the time expires.
  12. 12Unusual browser or webcam events may be recorded for administrative review.
  13. 13A detected event is not automatically proof of misconduct.

Important: A recorded event is an indicator for review, not a finding of guilt. Administrators consider context, repeated patterns and any explanation before drawing conclusions. See monitoring and privacy for details about what is and is not collected.

How the system works

From registration to published results, every step is recorded in the examination database.

01

02

Platform capabilities

Designed for fair, dependable digital assessment

Every component works together on Cloudflare Workers and a D1 database.

Secure authentication

Salted password hashing, HTTP-only session cookies, CSRF protection and role-based authorization.

Timed examinations

Start and end windows are validated on the server, and refreshing never resets your remaining time.

Question bank management

Administrators build multiple-choice questions with four options, marks and a defined order.

Automatic evaluation

Submissions are evaluated on the backend, producing marks, percentage and pass or fail status.

Webcam monitoring

With explicit consent, the browser checks for face presence and multiple faces using a local model.

Browser activity monitoring

Tab switches, loss of window focus and fullscreen exits are recorded as reviewable indicators.

Result management

Administrators release results when ready; students see only results that have been published.

Examination history

Students and administrators can review past attempts, scores and recorded proctoring events.

About the platform

A dependable examination environment

The Online Examination and Proctoring System is an institutional-style digital assessment platform. It brings student accounts, examination scheduling, question management, secure attempts and automatic evaluation into one consistent application.

Each attempt is tied to a server-recorded start time and a fixed deadline. Answers are stored against the attempt as the student proceeds, so a refresh or temporary connection problem does not erase progress. On submission, the backend evaluates the attempt and stores the result. Administrators decide when results become visible to students.

Browser-based monitoring is intentionally transparent. Students see the webcam preview, receive visible warnings, and must provide consent before a monitored examination starts. No webcam images or recordings are stored. Recorded events are presented to administrators for human review, with an explicit reminder that an event alone does not establish misconduct.

Principles

Authoritative backendTiming, evaluation and result calculation happen on the server, not in the browser.
Data minimisationNo biometric templates, webcam images or recordings are stored by this application.
Human reviewProctoring indicators are reviewed by administrators before any conclusion is drawn.
Accessible by designKeyboard-friendly controls, visible focus states and responsive layouts.
Monitoring and privacy

What monitoring can and cannot observe

Recorded with consent

  • Whether a face is visible in the webcam preview.
  • Whether more than one face appears in the preview.
  • When the camera stream stops or is disconnected.
  • Tab switches, loss of window focus and fullscreen exits.
  • Timestamps and a severity level for each event.

Not claimed or collected

  • Reliable detection of other applications, physical devices or external help.
  • Webcam images, audio or video recordings.
  • Facial recognition or stored biometric templates.
  • Silent camera activation of any kind.

A browser can only observe events it is permitted to see. Detected events may have innocent explanations and are provided to administrators as indicators, never as automatic verdicts.
